Subject focus

Keep the dots on the subject and thin them out behind it. Detect finds the background by flooding in from the edges you pick, so it works best on plain or softly blurred backgrounds.

Edges that show background

Leave off any edge the subject touches, such as the bottom of a portrait.

Higher treats more colours as background.

0% leaves the background blank. 100% ignores the mask.

Brush

Paint on the sheet, then release to update the dots. Lower the dot count while editing for quicker previews.

Dots

Up to 500,000. Above 200,000 the spacing step can take 10 to 30 seconds and the preview refreshes every few passes. Pair high counts with a small dot size.

0% keeps every dot the same size, so darkness comes only from density. Higher values also enlarge dots in dark areas.

Scatters each dot around its evenly spaced position, scaled to local density. 0 looks like a regular grain, 100 is nearly pure random.

Tone and edges

Sharpens fine patterns such as fabric folds and hair so they survive as dot structure.

Adds dots along contours. Too much flattens tonal gradation.
